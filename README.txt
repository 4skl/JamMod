JamMod brings synchronized multiplayer music and audio streaming directly into Minecraft.
Features

    Zero-Drift Multiplayer Sync: Uses server-authoritative timestamps to keep track position perfectly aligned for all listeners.

    Direct Audio Streaming: Streams audio dynamically from external links (such as YouTube) without requiring bloated resource packs.

    Spatial 3D Sound: Audio attenuates naturally in 3D space around in-world sound sources (speakers, boomboxes, or instruments).

    Late-Join Synchronization: Players entering audible range automatically seek and catch up to the currently playing track.

    Playback Controls: In-game interface for queuing, playing, pausing, and adjusting localized volume.
